Fitness Access

Step 2 · Statement of Understanding
Fitness Access is defined as unstaffed hours of operation. Access to the Eagle Fitness Center (EFC) during Fitness Access is a privilege that carries greater responsibility. Failure to comply with the following rules & conditions may result in suspension or termination of access.
Section I – Rules of Use
I must register my Common Access Card (CAC), sign this Statement of Understanding & Assumption of Risk Form, & receive a program briefing by EFC Staff prior to participating.
I understand there is no EFC staff during Fitness Access hours, & surveillance cameras will monitor activity.
I understand that only authorized patrons may access EFC during unstaffed hours, and I will not allow any customers to access the facility under my account. This includes holding or propping open doors to allow access. I acknowledge that video surveillance cameras monitor this activity.
I will ensure doors close securely after entering & exiting the facility.
I understand the risk of exercising alone and the buddy concept is highly encouraged.
I will assess risks & only use EFC equipment as it is intended to be used.
I will not remove safety devices from equipment & I will use safety collars on barbells
I will re-rack weights after use.
I will wear appropriate work out attire. Shoes & shirts must be worn at all times.
I will not record videos or take photos in the facility.
I will only play music through personal earphones/buds
I understand restricted areas will be locked or marked as off-limits. e.g., saunas & customer service desk.
In case of emergencies, I will use the phone at the Front Desk to contact NSA police, first responders, and/or EFC staff.
I understand the US Government, US Air Force, EFC, & associated personnel are not responsible for my personal property & I may be liable for any damage I cause to government property.
Section II – Violations & Disciplinary Actions
1st Violation: Verbal warning from EFC

2nd Violation: Suspension (30 days), & retraining before regaining access

3rd Violation: Suspension (90 days), written request from leadership to re-instate, & retraining
Section III – Assumption of Risk & Waiver of Claims
I assume all risks of injury and waive all rights to pursue monetary damages or any other relief of any kind because of injury or other loss occurring at or near the Eagle Health and Fitness Center. In the event I am injured while at the EFC, I will hold harmless the United States Government, the United States Air Force, EFC, any administrative subdivision or agency thereof, and the respective personnel and employees thereof from all claims of any sort for damages or for other relief. I understand and agree that the EFC will be unsupervised and that no personnel assigned to the Fitness Center or other employee will be on site to help me use the equipment or exercise in the manner that I choose to exercise. I acknowledge there is possible danger connected with any physical activity (including the dangers of physical injury and death) and knowingly and voluntarily waive my right to make legal or equitable claims of any sort against the United States Government, the United States Air Force, EFC administrative subdivision or agency thereof and the respective personnel and employees thereof. This assumption of risk and waiver of liability applies to my family members, successors, heirs and assigns.
Section IV – Preexisting Medical Conditions
I represent that I am in good physical health and have no symptoms, medical conditions, impairments, or diseases that might be aggravated, worsened, or induced by my intended use of the EFC. If I have any health or medical concerns now or after I register, I will immediately discontinue my use of the EFC until I am cleared for physical activity by a physician.
Section V – Orientation & Safety
I am familiar with safe operation of fitness equipment. If not, I will complete an orientation before after-hours use.
I received instructions on emergency procedures, phone usage, Automatic External Defibrillator & first aid kit.
